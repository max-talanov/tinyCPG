\subsection{Limitations and future work}
\todo{Mandatory subsection. Discuss:
\begin{enumerate}
  \item \textbf{Two legs, not four.} Our model omits V3 ascending
        long-propriospinal pathways and fore--hindlimb coordination
        --- the central topic of~\cite{zhang2022}. Extending to
        4 legs is straightforward (replicate the L/R hindlimb pair into
        a forelimb pair, add V3 aLPN-like diagonal synchronisation).
  \item \textbf{Single gait.} We pace one gait (paced trot at
        600--1400\,ms cycle); we do not reproduce walk$\to$trot$\to$
        gallop$\to$bound transitions. This is structural: our paced
        gait is externally driven, not self-emergent at varying
        descending-drive levels.
  \item \textbf{Izhikevich, not Hodgkin--Huxley.} We trade
        biophysical detail for simulation efficiency. The persistent
        sodium (INaP) dynamics are approximated by the intrinsic-
        bursting Izhikevich parameter set ($c=-55, d=4$). For
        questions specifically about INaP pharmacology our model is
        not appropriate; for questions about network-level rhythm
        organisation it is.
  \item \textbf{No statistical replication.} Our results are
        deterministic point estimates from fixed-seed runs; the
        $(\mu, \mathrm{CV})$ sweep substitutes for stochastic
        replication.
  \item \textbf{Self-organisation is local.} Only the descending
        BS$\to$RG and CUT$\to$RG synapses are plastic; the central
        reciprocal-inhibition and commissural weights are
        hand-tuned. A fully self-organising spinal CPG remains future
        work.
\end{enumerate}}
